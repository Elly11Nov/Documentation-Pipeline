# Content Intake

## Purpose

Content intake is the first stage of the documentation pipeline.

The purpose is to understand the source material before analysing, structuring or writing the documentation.

At this stage, the objective is not to write content. It is to establish:

- What information has been provided
- Where the information comes from
- What documentation may need to be produced
- What information is missing or unclear
- What questions may require clarification

This creates a clear boundary between **receiving information** and **interpreting information**.


## Intake Workflow

```text
Source Material
       ↓
Identify the Source
       ↓
Understand the Documentation Need
       ↓
Assess Available Information
       ↓
Identify Gaps and Ambiguities
       ↓
Record Questions
       ↓
Ready for Content Analysis
